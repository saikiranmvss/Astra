Method

How every number is produced, and how far you can trust it

How every number is produced

Nothing here is looked up from a panchangam or generated by AI. Each value is computed in your browser by a Python engine (running through WebAssembly) from date, time, latitude and longitude.

  1. Time: local time → UTC (IANA time-zone history, daylight saving changes inside a range are sent to the engine) → TAI (leap seconds) → TT. Before 1972, Delta T (Espenak–Meeus).
  2. Ephemeris: NASA JPL DE440 Chebyshev coefficients (unmodified), 1850–2149.
  3. Apparent place: light-time, solar light deflection, relativistic aberration; IAU 2006 precession, IAU 2000A nutation.
  4. Sidereal: λsid = λtrop − ALahiri(t), A propagated from its 1956 anchor.
  5. Panchanga: Tithi from E = λMoon − λSun in 12° steps; Nakshatra in 13°20′; Yoga from the sum; Karana from 6° half-tithis. Start/end times root-solved to about 1 s.
  6. Range searches (calendar, festivals, events, muhurta): body longitudes are sampled daily and interpolated with 8-point Lagrange (Moon error < 0.1″), then every boundary is root-solved. Long planetary scans (beyond 3 years) use 4-day sampling on the mean equinox and are good to minutes.
  7. Lunar months: Amanta, named by the Sun's sidereal sign at the starting New Moon; a month with no sankranti is Adhika.
  8. Festivals: each rule is (month, tithi, time-of-day window): sunrise, arunodaya, purvahna, madhyahna, aparahna, pradosha, nishitha or moonrise. The civil day with the most tithi coverage in that window is chosen. Ekadashi follows the Smarta rule, with the Vaishnava date and the Parana window also shown.
  9. Dashas: Vimshottari from the Moon's unelapsed nakshatra arc; Yogini from (nakshatra + 3) mod 8; Ashtottari from the 27-nakshatra groups starting at Ardra; Chara dasha per K. N. Rao. Sukshma and Prana levels are subdivided with the same proportions.
  10. Muhurta: day/night windows are intersected with the activity's tithi, nakshatra, weekday and lagna rules, minus Rahu kala, Yamaganda, Gulika, Durmuhurta, Varjyam, Vishti, bad yogas and Rikta tithis; Tarabala and Chandrabala if birth data is given.
  11. Transits: exact positions at the transit moment; gochara from the natal Moon with Phaladeepika vedha; Sade Sati from root-solved Saturn ingresses.
  12. Eclipses: New/Full Moons are screened by the Moon's argument of latitude; lunar contacts from the Danjon shadow, solar eclipses from the shadow axis (gamma) and topocentric local circumstances.
  13. Strength: Shadbala per BPHS (profile shown); Ashtakavarga raw bindus; Gulika/Mandi from the day/night eighths.
  14. Matching: Ashtakoota (36 points) and the South Indian 10 poruthams on calculated Moon nakshatras, plus Kuja dosha from lagna and Moon.
  15. Places: city search uses OpenStreetMap Nominatim only to find coordinates; the time zone comes from your browser's IANA database.

Validation

Positions agree with the independent Skyfield library to better than 0.001″; New Moons to 0.001 s; sunrise to about 2 s. Eclipse gammas and magnitudes match NASA's catalogue to about 0.005 (spherical Earth). 2027 festival dates for Hyderabad match the published Telugu calendars.

Accuracy you should expect

The astronomy is far more precise than any astrological boundary. The real limit is the birth time: the Lagna moves about 1° every 4 minutes. Values close to a boundary are flagged. Festival dates are regional conventions; the rule used is shown next to every date.
